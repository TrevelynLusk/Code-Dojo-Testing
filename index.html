Write code, run it, find out why it broke.

Twelve built-in lessons take you from your first console.log to a small program that totals a shopping cart. Everything runs on your phone. Nothing to install, no account, no sign-up.

There's also an AI instructor that can see your code and your errors and talk you through them. That part is optional and needs a free key of your own — about a minute to set up, and you can do it later.

Your code and progress are saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
